Hi! I am currently an Assistant Professor at the School of Electrical Engineering, Guangxi University. I received my Ph.D. and M.S. degrees in Electrical Engineering from Hunan University, advised by Prof. He Wen. During my doctoral studies, I was also a joint-training Ph.D. student at the Department of Industrial Engineering, University of Trento, advised by Prof. Dario Petri, Prof. David Macii, and Prof. Davide Brunelli. Prior to that, I obtained my B.S. degree in Electrical Engineering from Guangxi University.

My research interests revolve around the applications of machine learning and signal processing in smart grids, specifically focusing on non-intrusive load monitoring (NILM), AI in power electronics, and virtual power plants.
