Hi! I am currently an Assistant Professor at the School of Electrical Engineering, [Guangxi University](https://www.gxu.edu.cn/). I received my Ph.D. and M.S. degrees in Electrical Engineering from [Hunan University](https://www.hnu.edu.cn/), advised by Prof. [He Wen](https://eeit.hnu.edu.cn/info/1349/10841.htm). During my doctoral studies, I was also a joint-training Ph.D. student at the Department of Industrial Engineering, [University of Trento](https://www.unitn.it/en), advised by Prof. [Dario Petri](https://webapps.unitn.it/du/en/Persona/PER0004648/Curriculum), Prof. [David Macii](https://webapps.unitn.it/du/en/Persona/PER0003449/Didattica), and Prof. [Davide Brunelli](https://www.unibo.it/sitoweb/davide.brunelli/en). Prior to that, I obtained my B.S. degree in Electrical Engineering from Guangxi University.

My research interests revolve around the applications of machine learning and signal processing in smart grids, specifically focusing on non-intrusive load monitoring (NILM), AI in power electronics, and virtual power plants.

Please feel free to contact me if you are interested in my work or if you are seeking potential collaborations. [[Email](mailto:zhongzong.yan@gxu.edu.cn)]
